VINTAGE VOCODER - INSTALL
=========================

Two plugins are included. They sound the same; they plug into Ableton differently:
  Vintage Vocoder     instrument. Goes on a MIDI track; voice comes in via Sidechain.
  Vintage Vocoder FX  audio effect. Goes on the vocal track; MIDI comes in via "MIDI To".
See docs/ableton-setup.md in the repository for step-by-step routing.

macOS
-----
  VST3:  copy the .vst3 bundles to      ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
  AU:    copy the .component bundles to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
  The builds are not notarised, so remove the download quarantine once (Terminal):
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Vintage*.vst3
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Vintage*.component
  The Standalone "Vintage Vocoder FX.app" runs without a DAW (mic in + MIDI keyboard).

Windows
-------
  Copy the .vst3 folders to   C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
  "Vintage Vocoder FX.exe" is the standalone app.

Ableton Live
------------
  Preferences > Plug-Ins: enable "Use VST3 Plug-in System Folders" (and "Use Audio Units" on macOS),
  then press Rescan. The plugins appear under Plug-Ins > saranunt.
